Building Again
Pavasa
A premium D2C brass home & lifestyle brand.

After years of building products inside organizations, I'm now product lead on taking my family's brass manufacturing business direct to consumer. Sourcing, manufacturing, design language, brand and commerce — built end to end, on top of a manufacturing heritage that was already there.

This section isn't here to sell Pavasa. It's here to show what it means to have skin in the game — to make decisions across an entire business, not just a product.

The commerce platform is one I'm building myself — storefront, catalogue, payments and courier serviceability checks that stop undeliverable orders before they're placed. Pre-launch, the existing B2B customer base has already placed a 100-unit opening order for hooks and booked three high-value brass chess boards, with inbound interest in a wall-decor line that hasn't been released yet.

The journey, so far

Every material choice, every product photograph, every price decision — mine to make and mine to live with. It's the same discipline as product work, without the safety net of a larger organisation behind it.

Inside the build

What building it alone actually involves.

Not a side project with a landing page. A live system with backups, monitoring, courier integration and an accounting trail — built, run and decided on by one person.
